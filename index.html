About me

I’m a software engineer who likes working close to the hard parts of a product: its data model, APIs, deployment, and failure modes.

My work has moved through enterprise banking, analytics products, cloud infrastructure, and applied AI. Today, I build retrieval and tool-calling systems at FL Tech Solutions. Outside work, I’m developing Qorthos, a local-first runtime that checks AI-generated code against the schemas, contracts, migrations, infrastructure, and business rules it is supposed to respect.

I care about useful software, legible systems, and evidence over demos. I’m most at home on teams where engineers understand the product, make careful tradeoffs, and remain responsible for what happens after launch.

What I’m looking for

Backend, platform, full-stack, or applied-AI work with meaningful ownership—especially problems involving reliability, developer infrastructure, data-intensive systems, or AI evaluation.
